FAQs

What is business coaching in India?
Business coaching helps business owners think clearly, improve systems, and make better decisions to grow faster.
How is it different from business consulting services?
Coaching focuses on empowering the owner, while consulting often involves doing the work for the business.
Who should hire a business coach?
Any MSME owner looking to scale, systemize, or move out of the daily 'firefighting' phase of their business.
