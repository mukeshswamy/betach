Leadership Safety Legacy
"Whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant."
— Matthew 20:26

BETACH Consulting Services was founded with a simple but powerful vision: to create a legacy of leadership that transforms workplaces, protects lives, and positively impacts future generations.

After spending more than 15 years working across Oil & Gas, Construction, and Industrial projects in the Middle East, I witnessed something that changed my perspective forever. While systems, procedures, and technology are important, the greatest factor influencing safety, performance, and people's lives is leadership.

Throughout my journey on project sites and within corporate environments, I observed that every decision made by a supervisor, manager, or executive has a direct impact on the people working under and around them. Strong leadership creates trust, accountability, and a culture where people thrive. Poor leadership, however, can lead to confusion, disengagement, incidents, and missed opportunities.

This realization became the foundation of BETACH.

The name BETACH represents our commitment to building leaders who take ownership, lead with integrity, and genuinely care for the well-being of others. We believe that while some people may be natural leaders, leadership is a skill that can be learned, developed, and strengthened through the right mindset, coaching, and experience.

At BETACH, we don't just teach safety—we develop leaders. Leaders who influence positive behavior, create safer workplaces, inspire their teams, and make better decisions both at work and in life.

Because leadership is not about position or authority.

"Leadership is the responsibility to influence, protect, and serve others."

As leadership expert John C. Maxwell said:

"A leader is one who knows the way, goes the way, and shows the way."

Our mission is to equip individuals and organizations with the leadership skills needed to build strong safety cultures, improve performance, and ensure that every person returns home safely to their family.

We are not just building a company.

"We are building a legacy of leaders who make a difference wherever they go."
