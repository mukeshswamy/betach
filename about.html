About Betach

Empowering India's MSMEs to transform from firefighting to sustainable growth through expert business coaching.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help 1 million MSME business owners build impactful businesses and become industry leaders. We believe that MSMEs are the backbone of the Indian economy, and our goal is to provide them with the systems, strategies, and mindset needed to scale.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and impactful business coaching organization in India, recognized for transforming traditional businesses into professionally managed, high-growth enterprises.

The Betach Story

Founded with a passion for entrepreneurship, Betach began its journey 18 years ago. We noticed a recurring pattern among MSME owners: they were working FOR their business rather than ON it.

We realized that while these owners had the passion and product knowledge, they often lacked the management systems and growth frameworks required to scale. This insight led to the creation of our signature coaching programs.

Today, with a team of over 70 in-house coaches, we have impacted thousands of lives across 196+ industries. Our approach is hands-on, practical, and results-driven.
